FAQ

Questions, answered

Is TripSarthi only for travel agents?

It’s designed for Indian travel agencies, tour operators and DMCs — quotes, itineraries, supplier rates, group departures, TCS and travel-specific automations are all built in. If you run a WhatsApp-led sales business, the CRM and inbox are useful on their own, too.

Do I need the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes — TripSarthi works with the official WhatsApp Business Platform using your own number and Meta account, so you stay in control. We enforce the 24-hour window and approved-template rules on every message. Meta bills conversation charges directly to you; TripSarthi adds no per-message markup.

Will the AI make up prices?

No. The AI helps draft itineraries, replies and ad copy, but a price can only come from a rate card you’ve entered. Imported rate sheets are previewed row by row, and any price the AI can’t find in your text is flagged instead of saved.

How does GST invoicing work?

Add your business profile and GSTIN once. TripSarthi issues tax invoices or bills of supply with the right CGST/SGST/IGST split, keeps numbering gap-free, and exports GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and HSN summaries for your CA. E-invoicing (IRN) is available for businesses above the turnover threshold through your GST Suvidha Provider. Please have your CA confirm settings before you go live.

Can I import my suppliers and rates?

Yes. Upload an Excel or CSV file — or paste text from an email — and review the preview before anything is saved. Existing rates are updated in place so older quotes keep working.

Which ad platforms are supported?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads. Campaigns are created paused, a daily spend cap is mandatory, and you connect your own ad accounts. Reporting joins ad spend to the bookings and revenue each campaign produced.

Is my customers’ data safe?

Each agency’s data is isolated from others. Passport numbers and customer document uploads are encrypted at rest, ad audiences are built only from contacts who opted in and are hashed before upload, and you decide who on your team sees costs and margins.

Is there a mobile app?

A mobile app for your team — push alerts, WhatsApp inbox, quick enquiry and quotes — is being rolled out for iOS and Android. Customers use a mobile-friendly web portal, so they don’t need to install anything.

Can I try it before paying?

Create a free account and explore with your own data. Choose a paid plan when you’re ready to connect your WhatsApp number and invite your team.
